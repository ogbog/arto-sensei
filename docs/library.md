# The public-domain library

Arto Sensei's library feeds sight-size copies (lesson 16, the capstone) and the Dojo's timed figure drawing.
Every downloaded image is filtered to **public domain or CC0 license metadata**, and its title, artist,
license and source page are saved in that folder's `credits.json`. **Copy credit** puts that line on the
clipboard.

Library folder: `<Krita app data>/arto_sensei/library/` (Windows: `%APPDATA%\krita\arto_sensei\library`).
Any folder you create there, full of your own JPGs or PNGs, shows up as a set too.

## Sources

| Source | When | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Wikimedia Commons | Always | Paintings, drawings, engravings, photos of sculpture. Standard 1280 px images. |
| Openverse | If you connected an Openverse account in **Photobash CC** (Arto Sensei reuses that login) | Museum collections: the Met, Smithsonian, Rijksmuseum and more. Filtered with `license=pdm,cc0`. |

## Pruned from the wishlist, and why

Public domain means old. Roughly: the artist died before 1956 (life plus 70 years, the rule in most of Europe), or, for US work, it was published before 1931. Living artists can't be downloaded from these sources, so searching for them only returns noise.
